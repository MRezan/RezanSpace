The small models having a very big moment

Why the next phase of AI may be defined by what fits in your pocket—not what fills a data center.

M
Maya ChenAugust 2, 2026 · 8 min read
Discuss
REZAN / TECHNOLOGY

For years, progress in artificial intelligence was measured in scale: more parameters, more data, more computing power. That equation is changing quickly. A new generation of compact models is proving that focused intelligence can be more useful than raw size.

These models can run locally, respond instantly and keep sensitive information on-device. That combination matters for healthcare, finance, education and every other field where latency and privacy are not optional features.

The winners will not necessarily build the largest brain. They will build the right intelligence for the moment, and put it exactly where people need it.
